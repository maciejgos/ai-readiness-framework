---
title: Operational AI pilot playbook
date: 2025-02-12
summary: A lightweight checklist for running a 6–8 week AI pilot with measurable ROI and guardrails.
---

## Start with one measurable process

Pick a process that repeats weekly, has a clear owner, and produces data you can measure. Examples include support triage, invoice prep, or responding to RFPs. Define the KPI you want to improve and the current baseline.

- What is the weekly volume?
- Who signs off on the output today?
- How long does the work take?

## Run in your tenant with human-in-the-loop controls

Set up the pilot inside your Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace tenant. Limit access to one functional team and log every action so you can audit outcomes.

1. Create service accounts with least-privilege permissions.
2. Store prompts, configs, and logs in a shared project folder.
3. Define an escalation path for edge cases.

## Measure outcomes every week

Share a simple weekly dashboard that tracks adoption and quality. Compare the pilot week-over-week to the baseline and capture anecdotes from the team.

- Turnaround time per item
- Accuracy or rework rate
- Team confidence and comments

If the numbers move in the right direction, expand to the next process with the same playbook.
